📜 Submission Guidelines & Rules

All demonlist operations are carried out in accordance with our guidelines. Please read carefully before submitting a level or record!

1. Level Eligibility

Levels do not have to be tiny! You can submit any level that you have beaten or verified (Hard Demon or harder).

2. Raw Footage & Audio Clicks

Real-time audio clicks or taps are strictly required for all submissions. No click mods, macros, or edited audio allowed.

3. Proof Video Quality

Video proof must show full unedited gameplay from level start to end screen with clear video quality (720p or higher recommended).

4. Allowed Modifications

Standard cosmetic physics/FPS bypass mods are permitted, but hack menus, speedhacks, or frame-steppers will result in an immediate ban.
